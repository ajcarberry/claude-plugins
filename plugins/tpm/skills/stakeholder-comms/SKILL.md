# Stakeholder Comms — Same Facts, Different Altitude

One set of facts, re-rendered per audience. Comms **never invents facts** — it only
selects, orders, and altitudes what the roadmap, decisions, and feedback record
already say. If a claim isn't in an artifact, it doesn't go in an update.

## The audience model

**Community** (Discord, GitHub discussions) — owed openness:
- What changed and **the reasoning** — decisions communicated with their why;
  a conclusion without reasoning reads as dismissal.
- What happens next and when to expect it.
- Where their feedback went: "you said X, here's what we did with it" closes the
  loop that keeps people reporting.
- Credit contributors by name for shipped work and good reports.
- Honest about what was declined and why — silence is worse than no.

**Executive sponsors** — owed signal density:
- TL;DR first: on track / at risk / blocked, in one line.
- Progress against **their** commitments, not activity ("closed 40 issues" is
  activity; "search ships on schedule" is progress).
- Risks with a stated ask or a stated plan — a risk without either is just worry.
- Decisions needed from them, each with a recommendation and a decide-by date.

## Rules

- Bad news identically in both renderings. Only the altitude changes — never the
  facts. If the community learns something sponsors weren't told (or vice versa),
  that's a comms failure.
- Cite the artifacts (roadmap link, decision record, theme doc) so readers can go
  deeper than the update.
- **Draft only — never post or send without the user's OK.** Output is a draft in
  the appropriate template: [community-update](../../templates/community-update.md)
  or [exec-update](../../templates/exec-update.md).
