# TPM Landing Mechanics

For product work, "deploy" is **publish** and the landing checks are **sign-offs and
comprehension**, not telemetry.

## Deploy = Publish

- Put the doc where its audience actually reads (the wiki/Drive/Notion space they
  use, not where the repo is convenient), with a one-paragraph summary at the top —
  most stakeholders read only that.
- Announce in the audience's channel with the decision needed and the deadline —
  "FYI" posts land nothing.

## Validate — the sign-off checklist

- **Named approvers have explicitly approved** — silence is not approval; a list of
  names with checkmarks is the landing evidence.
- **The skeptic reviewed it** — the person most likely to disagree saw it *before*
  broad distribution, not after.
- **Comprehension check** — at least one reader can restate the decision and its
  main consequence back to you correctly. If they can't, the doc failed regardless
  of approvals.
- Open questions all have owners; none silently dropped between draft and publish.

## Rollback

A published decision that turns out wrong gets a visible amendment or a superseding
doc — never a silent edit. The record of what was decided (and then changed) is the
point.
